Supply Chain Management

Recovering a Fractured Supply Chain in Defense Electronics

Challenge

A tier-1 defense electronics manufacturer faced severe supplier fragmentation across 14 critical commodity families. Procurement lead times had extended to 28+ weeks, buffer stocks were depleted, and on-time delivery to program milestones had dropped below 61%.

Actions Taken

ZAPPY AI deployed a cross-functional supply chain recovery program: supplier segmentation, dual-sourcing protocols for high-risk components, and a centralized demand-signal system connecting procurement to production scheduling in real time.

Outcomes

  • 38% reduction in average procurement lead time within 6 months
  • +24 pts improvement in on-time delivery to program milestones
  • 2× increase in qualified alternate suppliers per critical commodity
  • $2.1M in annualized inventory carrying cost reduction

Procurement Optimization

Structural Cost Reduction Across an Industrial Procurement Portfolio

Challenge

A mid-sized industrial manufacturer operating across three production sites had accumulated a fragmented supplier base of 340+ vendors with no category management discipline. Procurement costs were 18% above benchmark, and maverick spend accounted for nearly a third of total purchasing volume.

Actions Taken

We executed a full spend-cube analysis, introduced category management across 8 strategic commodity families, rationalized the vendor base by 42%, and negotiated consolidated volume agreements with preferred suppliers — integrating new SLA frameworks and performance scorecards.

Outcomes

  • 14.6% net procurement cost reduction in Year 1
  • 42% vendor base rationalization improving contract leverage
  • –31% maverick spend through category governance rollout
  • 96% of preferred suppliers meeting new SLA thresholds
Manufacturing Operations

Stabilizing Subcontracting Operations in Medical Device Manufacturing

Challenge

A medical device OEM relying on a network of subcontractors for precision machining and assembly experienced cascading delivery failures. Subcontractor quality escapes had increased 3× year-over-year, creating regulatory exposure and threatening two major program deliveries.

Actions Taken

ZAPPY AI embedded a dedicated subcontracting management team on-site, restructured subcontractor qualification requirements, introduced first-article inspection protocols, and deployed a real-time production visibility dashboard linked to the client's ERP — enabling proactive escalation before schedule slippage occurred.

Outcomes

  • –78% quality escape rate within 90 days of engagement
  • +19 pts improvement in subcontractor on-time delivery rate
  • 100% on-time delivery achieved for both critical program milestones
  • 3.2 wks average lead time reduction across precision machining suppliers
